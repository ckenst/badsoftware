# The Bad Software Company - Advisory

Source: https://badsoftware.com/advisory.html

Summary: Evidence-based guidance for deciding what to change, what to defer, and how to measure whether the response is working.

Turn findings into decisions.

Advisory is a standalone service. It helps teams interpret what they are seeing, choose a response, sequence the work, and define useful feedback.

Work can include quality and testing strategy, product and architecture reviews, AI-assisted development, Developer Relations, workshops, training, conference talks, and executive briefings.
