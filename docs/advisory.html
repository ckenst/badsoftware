Advisory

Turn findings into decisions.

Advisory is a standalone service. It helps leaders interpret the evidence, choose a response, and sequence the work when knowing what is wrong does not make the next decision obvious.

Decisions grounded in the system you actually have

Advisory can follow a TestOpsy or address a focused decision. The work starts with the available evidence, the constraints your team faces, and the outcomes you need—not a generic maturity model.

Together, we clarify tradeoffs, separate urgent work from merely visible work, and define the feedback that will show whether a change is helping.

Advisory work can include

Quality strategy

Testing strategy, release confidence, quality signals, and the systems supporting them.

Product and architecture review

Independent review of technical direction, product assumptions, architecture, and delivery risk.

AI and Developer Relations

Practical guidance on AI-assisted development, developer experience, documentation, APIs, and SDKs.

Training and communication

Hands-on workshops, team training, conference talks, and executive briefings.
